## App name

Inbox

## Short description

A calm Gmail inbox: mark done, snooze, pin, and schedule mail to send later.

## Full description

Inbox is a simpler way to read your Gmail. It works on your existing Gmail account; your mail stays
in Gmail, and everything you do shows up in Gmail too.

• Done: clear a thread from your inbox with one swipe. It stays in All Mail.
• Snooze: hide a thread until a time you pick. It comes back on its own, or sooner if someone
  replies. Suggested times learn from the ones you choose.
• Pin: keep the threads you need at the top.
• Schedule send and undo send. Scheduled mail goes out even when your phone is off.
• Write in plain text with simple formatting, with your signature and contact suggestions.
• Search with Gmail's search, plus filters and labels.
• Remote images are off until you show them, for a message or for a sender.
• One-tap unsubscribe where the sender supports it.
• Answer calendar invitations from the message.
• Optional @hafa.cc address that forwards to your Gmail, and sending from it.
• Notifications with Done and Reply.
• Also on the web at inbox.hafa.cc, kept in sync.

No ads and no analytics. Privacy policy: https://inbox.hafa.cc/privacy

Inbox is an independent app and isn't made by Google. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC.

  transit:
  - Personal info › Email address (account sign-in, @hafa.cc forwarding).
