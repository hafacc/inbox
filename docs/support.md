# Support

Questions or problems with Inbox, on the web or on Android? Email support@inbox.hafa.cc, from your
Gmail address if it's about your account.

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/hafacc/inbox/issues/new).
Inbox is open source; its code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/hafacc/inbox).

## Signing in

Google says Inbox isn't verified. That's expected for a small app: choose **Advanced**, then
continue. Inbox asks for Gmail, Contacts and Calendar; the [privacy policy](/privacy/) says what
each is for.

## Your mail

Inbox shows the mail in your Gmail account and changes it there, so anything you archive, label or
send in Inbox is the same in Gmail. Signing out or deleting your Inbox account never deletes mail.

## Deleting your account

Delete your account in Settings (under General on the web). If you can't sign in any more, email
support@inbox.hafa.cc from your Gmail address and everything the server stores about you is deleted
within 30 days.
